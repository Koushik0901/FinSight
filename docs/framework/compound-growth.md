# Compound Growth

> Time is the exponent. Small amounts, compounded, become the plan. — *Hill* / *Kiyosaki*

## The projection

FinSight renders a **10 / 20 / 30-year projection at 7% annual** from the current contribution rate:

> “At $500/month, 7% annual, ~$86k in 10 years before inflation.”

It is deterministic arithmetic in `metrics` / Goals, not a market prediction. The Copilot is instructed to say so and to avoid implying guarantees.

## Where it appears

- **Goals** — each funded goal shows the three horizons live as you change the monthly amount.
- **Budget forecast** — short-range (this month) vs long-range (decades) are kept separate; the forecast explains burn rate, the projection explains compounding.
- **Copilot** — “What does $200/month extra do over 20 years?” returns the delta across horizons with the same 7% assumption.

## Using it

Adjust the monthly amount on a goal and watch the long-range figure move. That instant feedback is the point: the habit matters more than the exact rate, and the habit is yours to tune.

The 7% is a common planning assumption, not a promise. Inflation and fees are not modelled; the inspector makes the assumption explicit.
