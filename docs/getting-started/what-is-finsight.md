# What is FinSight?

FinSight is not a hosted SaaS, a stock picker, or a marketing dashboard. It is a calm, local-first ledger with an AI copilot that helps you make the next good financial decision.

## The core idea

1. **You own the ledger.** Accounts, transactions, budgets, categories, goals — all live in an encrypted per-user SQLite database on your server. The browser is a viewer, not the vault.
2. **AI is opt-in and replaceable.** Categorization and Copilot use the provider you configure, or none at all. Local Ollama keeps inference on your machine; remote providers receive only the redacted context needed for a task.
3. **Principles guide behaviour.** Six ideas from *Babylon, Ramsey, Sethi, Housel, Hill, Kiyosaki* are encoded in the wellness context and system prompt — not as motivational copy, but as calculations (savings rate, emergency-fund months, snowball order, allocation).

## Screens you will use

- **Today** — net worth, income/expenses, savings rate, category stream, and one clear next action.
- **Copilot** — conversational planning with streaming, typed finance cards, and reviewable action bundles.
- **Insights** — anomalies, patterns, memory, and a needs-review feed.
- **Accounts** — manual and SimpleFIN-synced accounts, balance history, net worth.
- **Transactions** — search, filters, review queues, transfers, splits, drawer editing.
- **Budget** — envelope planning, To Budget, carryover, and Conscious Spending allocation.
- **Categories** — groups, guidance, and spending types (Need/Want/Saving/Investment).
- **Recurring**, **Goals**, **Reports**, **Scenarios**, **Journey**, **Recipes**, **Rules**, **Settings**.

## Philosophy in practice

> “A quiet way to understand, plan, and master your money.”

That means fewer totals, more decisions. Surfaces are flat and calm; lime (`#C9F950`) is reserved for state and action. The app marks financial figures with `money` so privacy mode can blur them.

## Who it is for

- People who want a self-hosted alternative to cloud budget apps.
- Households that share a server but keep separate encrypted databases.
- Anyone who wants AI help without handing the ledger to a vendor.

If you want confetti, streaks, and piles of coins, this is the wrong product. FinSight is an instrument — check it, make one good decision, close it.
