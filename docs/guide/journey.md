# Journey

Seven milestones from stability to freedom — where you are, what unlocks next, and the Copilot entry points that get you there.

## The milestones

| # | Milestone | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stability | Bills paid, income tracked, categories set |
| 2 | Emergency buffer | 1 month of expenses saved |
| 3 | Emergency fund | 3–6 months saved (Ramsey/Sethi) |
| 4 | Debt free | Non-mortgage debt cleared, snowball order followed |
| 5 | On track | Savings rate ≥10% for several months; allocation healthy |
| 6 | Compounding | Goals funded, 10/20/30-year projection is credible |
| 7 | Freedom | Investments + cash cover years of expenses; Behaviour > math is the focus |

Progress is computed from `wellness_context` and goal/budget state. A milestone unlocks when its thresholds are met against your actual ledger — the Copilot will not mark it complete on promises.

## Using Journey

- Click a milestone → Copilot opens with a grounded prompt (“How do I reach Emergency Fund with my current burn rate?”).
- The progress bar animates on reveal (respects `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- “More” disclosure holds secondary detail — primary milestones stay scannable.

Journey is a motivator, not a scoreboard. The underlying math is exposed in the metric inspector whenever you want to check it.
